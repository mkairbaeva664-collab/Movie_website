Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is MovieHub free to use?
+

Yes! MovieHub is completely free for browsing our movie catalog, watching official trailers, reading reviews, and managing your personal movie ratings.

2. Can I watch full movies directly on MovieHub?
+

No, MovieHub is a movie discovery, rating, and review platform. We provide detailed film descriptions, user ratings, and official YouTube trailers to help you choose what to watch next.

3. How do I add or edit movies in the Ratings table?
+

Go to the Ratings page in the navigation menu and click the "+ Add Movie" button. You can enter a title, genre, and rating (0–10), as well as edit or delete existing entries at any time.

4. Why do I need to create an account on MovieHub?
+

Creating an account personalizes your experience on MovieHub, keeping you signed in across sessions so you can participate in the community and share your movie opinions.

5. How often is the movie catalog updated?
+

We regularly update our catalog and trailers section with both timeless classics and trending releases across Action, Comedy, Drama, Horror, and Romance genres.
